Love & Hate Discord?

We all share a love-hate relationship with Discord. With millions of active users every day, Discord has grown into a massive platform, yet it often fails to listen to its own community. Features that users have been begging for for years are ignored, while unnecessary updates are forced upon us.

Disc-Tools was born out of this frustration. We wanted to show Discord that the community is more than just a metric – we are capable, creative, and we won't just sit by while our needs are overlooked. This project is a statement: the community matters, and if the platform won't provide the tools we need, we'll build them ourselves.

This entire platform was envisioned and built by a single individual, kebab (also known as mistr_kebab), with the goal of empowering every Discord user and developer with the utilities they deserve.

Security First
We believe in privacy. That's why our sensitive tools perform all processing locally in your browser. Your data, your control.
Developer Focused
Built by developers for developers. We use the official Discord API to provide accurate and real-time information.
Always Free
Our core tools will always remain free and accessible to everyone in the community. No hidden costs.
