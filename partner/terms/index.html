Partner Terms

Last updated: September 12, 2026 — Effective immediately

These Partner Terms govern the Disc-Tools Partner Program. They supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. By applying for, accepting or maintaining Partner status you agree to these Terms.

TL;DR: Partnership is free, non-exclusive, revocable and mutual. We list you as a trusted project — you keep your server active, moderated and legal, publish our ad verbatim (only our ad from /ad-text.txt — use the Copy our Advertisement button on /partner/) in a staff-only channel within 24h and ping the @Partnership role (or @everyone if the role was just created — we do not ping for you, just ping the role, not yourself), keep a permanent opt-in ping role, and we both keep Discord ToS. Zero tolerance: sexualization of minors, hate or illegal content and failure to ban offenders leads to immediate termination without warning. No payments unless separately agreed in writing.

Partnership does not create a company, agency, employment or joint venture. Neither party may legally represent the other.

2. Eligibility

To be eligible you must at the time of application and throughout the partnership:

  • Operate a Discord-related project, community, bot, tool or service that is active and publicly accessible (website, Discord invite, GitHub, etc.).
  • Be at least 13 years old and comply with Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Discord's Developer Terms.
  • Own or be authorized to represent the project (no impersonation).
  • Have a project that does not violate German law (incl. StGB, JuSchG, TMG/DDG) or our Terms of Service — in particular no spam, phishing, token grabbing, cracked clients, pay-to-win harassment, hate, extremism, illegal gambling or adult content targeting minors.
  • Maintain a reasonable quality standard: working invite/website, no deceptive advertising, no excessive downtime, staffed moderation.

We review each application individually. There is no entitlement to be accepted, and we do not have to give reasons for rejection. Criteria may include relevance, quality, community size, activity and reputation.

3. Application & approval

  • Apply via disc-tools.de/partner/request/ while logged in with Discord and as a member of our Discord server discord.gg/rtRs8rhj5u (required for verification & communication). Our backend also verifies membership.
  • You must submit accurate information (project name, website and/or Discord invite, description, logo if any). We may ask for additional proof.
  • By submitting the form you confirm you have read and accepted these Partner Terms (checkbox is mandatory and enforced server-side).
  • If accepted, we create a partner profile (name, description, logo, links, slug) on /partner/. You are responsible for notifying us about material changes (rebrand, new domain, ownership change).
  • Approval is personal to your project and non-transferable without our written consent.

4. What partners get

Unless individually agreed otherwise, benefits may include:

  • A featured card on /partner/ and a public detail page (with logo, description and links).
  • Optional Discord role / badge on our server and priority support via partner channels.
  • Occasional co-promotion (e.g. in announcements, social posts, or the tool directory) — at our sole discretion, no guaranteed reach. Announcements on our server will generally use our opt-in Partnership ping role, not @everyone.

All benefits are voluntary and may be changed or discontinued at any time without compensation.

5. What we expect from partners

5.1 General obligations

  • Keep it clean & alive: Keep your project online, moderated and free of illegal or harmful content. Fix broken invites/websites promptly. Ensure staff coverage (mods/admins) and an active reporting path for users.
  • No misrepresentation: Do not claim to be Disc-Tools, to be owned by us, or to be an official Discord partner unless you separately are. Do not use our name/logo in a way that suggests endorsement beyond the partnership.
  • Respect users & law: No spam/DM advertising using our name, no scraping our site beyond normal use, no bypassing rate limits or security measures. Respect GDPR and get user consent where required on your own properties.
  • Mandatory advertisement — mutual, verbatim, in a staff-only channel (you ping the role, §5.3): Within 24 hours of approval you must publish our advertisement verbatim and unmodified — and only our advertisement (no edited, shortened, translated, or paraphrased version) in a dedicated, non-volatile channel on your server where only staff can write — not all members (e.g. #partners, #partnerships, #partner-showcase with Send Messages denied for @everyone and allowed only for your Staff/Team roles), so the post remains permanently visible and is not immediately pushed down by general chat. Use the exact text from disc-tools.de/ad-text.txt at the time of approval (or copy it via the Copy our Advertisement button on /partner/) — __**Disc-Tools — Free Tools for Discord**__ ... https://disc-tools.de/ — without adding, removing or altering wording, links, or formatting beyond Discord's native rendering. You ping the role, not yourself: include the @Partnership role mention (or your equivalent opt-in role per §5.3) if the role already existed before the partnership; if the role was freshly created for this partnership and currently has 0 members, ping @everyone (or @here with our prior approval) for this initial post so users actually discover us — we do not ping on your behalf, just ping the role. Do not delete the post before the partnership ends (you may pin it). This partnership is based on mutuality — we will likewise showcase you on our server/site (listing) while you handle your own pings; if you remove or modify our ad or fail to ping the role as required, you are in breach and we may suspend the partnership per §9.
  • Stay reachable: Respond to our messages within a reasonable time (14 days) and keep your Discord contact up to date.
  • Notify changes: Tell us before a major rebrand, ownership transfer or if your project shuts down, violates Discord ToS, or receives serious abuse reports.

5.2 Community moderation & Discord ToS — Zero tolerance

Your community must comply at all times with Discord ToS, Community Guidelines and applicable German/EU law. You are responsible for moderation of your own server, website, bot, and linked communities.

  • Active moderation required: Maintain clear rules, active moderators, AutoMod / filters, and a functioning report/ban system. Do not tolerate or downplay violations.
  • Zero tolerance — immediate ban: The following lead to mandatory immediate action and, if ignored, to immediate termination per §9 without warning:
    • Sexualization of minors, grooming, CSAM, age-gap sexual content, or statements like “I love little kids” in a sexual context, even as a “joke”.
    • Hate speech, extremism, Holocaust denial, racial slurs, encouragement of self-harm, doxxing, or threats of violence.
    • Distribution of illegal content (StGB §§86, 130, 176 ff., BtMG, etc.), cracked Discord clients, token grabbers, malware, phishing.
    • Spamming, raiding, or harassing other communities using the partnership as cover.
  • Ban, don't warn: For the zero-tolerance categories above you must immediately ban the offender (no timeout-only, no “verbal warning”, no mute) and remove the content. Document the action (audit log). Failure to ban within 24 hours of becoming aware, or unbanning without proper appeal review, counts as a violation.
  • Cooperation: On request you must provide evidence of moderation (audit log, ban reason) within 48 hours. If we report a violating user/message to you, you must act within 24 hours.
  • No safe space for offenders: Knowingly keeping such users, giving them roles/staff, or publicly defending the statements is treated as endorsement and triggers immediate termination.

Example: User writes “I love little kids” (or any sexual statement about minors) in your server and you do not ban them, or you only timeout/warn — this is an immediate, no-warning termination of the partnership per §9. We report such cases to Discord Trust & Safety where required by law.

5.3 Ping / announcement role obligation — ping the role, no gaming the system

You are solely responsible for all pings on your own server — we do not ping on your behalf. You ping the role, not yourself. You must:

  • Mandatory self-assignable ping role: Maintain a dedicated, mentionable ping role on your Discord server (e.g. Partnership, Partnership Ping, Partner News, Pings — Partnerships) that any member can freely assign and remove themselves at any time — via Roles / Onboarding, Reaction Roles, Button Roles, or a bot command like /roles. The role must be clearly named and documented in your rules or role-info channel. A channel-follow or event-subscribe does not replace the role.
  • Must stay permanently: The role must remain configured even when 0 members have it. You may not delete the role when it is empty and recreate it only for an announcement, nor hide it, make it unmentionable, or move it to a private category to avoid pings. This is considered gaming the system.
  • Mentionable — but only by your team: The role must be mentionable by your team / announcement bot at posting time, but not by @everyone. Disable Allow anyone to mention this role and grant the mention permission only to your Team/Staff roles (or rely on the staff-only channel — only your team can send messages there, so only your team can actually ping the role). Do not leave the role mentionable by everyone.
  • Ping the role — fallback to @everyone: For the mandatory advertisement per §5.1 and any future partnership announcements on your server, you must mention the ping role (not yourself). If at that time no valid opt-in role exists, it was deleted while empty and recreated, is not self-assignable, is not mentionable, or has been disabled to dodge the ping, you must ping @everyone (or @here with our prior approval) instead — still you ping the role/everyone, we do not ping for you. Repeated gaming (≥2 incidents) is a breach and can lead to termination per §9.
  • Proof on request: We may ask for a screenshot or invite to verify the role exists and is self-assignable. Failure to provide proof within 7 days is treated as non-compliance.

Need help setting it up? In Server Settings → Roles → Create Role disable Allow anyone to mention this role and grant your Team role the permission to mention it (or keep the channel staff-only so only your team can send the ping) → Save → add the role to Onboarding or a Reaction Role bot so members can opt-in/out themselves. Only your team must be able to ping this role — members must not be able to ping it. Contact us if you need a template.

6. Our obligations & rights

  • We will host your partner profile with reasonable care and keep the information you provided reasonably up to date.
  • Mutual advertisement (listing only, you handle your own pings): We will showcase you on our Discord server and/or site (e.g. listing on /partner/ and a staff-only partnership showcase). All pings on your server are your responsibility alone — we do not ping on external servers. We handle the listing; you handle your channel and your pings.
  • We may edit descriptions for length/clarity, optimize logos, or move your card in the listing for editorial reasons — without changing the meaning.
  • We may monitor partner links, server compliance, ping-role availability, and whether the mandatory advertisement per §5.1 is still present/unmodified, and temporarily hide a listing if your site/server is offline, compromised, the ad was removed/altered, violates §5, or is flagged for abuse until clarified.
  • We are not responsible for your content, support requests or community management. Support for your users stays with you.
  • We reserve the right to audit your server for §5.1 (ad), §5.2 and §5.3 compliance (public channels / invite) at any time without notice.

7. Branding & trademark

  • Each party keeps all rights to its own name, logo and content. No license is granted except as needed to fulfill the partnership (e.g. we may display your logo on /partner/; you may use the text “Disc-Tools Partner” and our logo where we explicitly allow it).
  • If we provide a logo pack or brand guidelines, follow them. Do not distort, recolor beyond recognition, or place our mark in a way that implies superiority over Discord.
  • Any use beyond the partnership (merch, paid ads, domain names containing “disc-tools”) requires prior written permission.

8. No compensation & no exclusivity

Partnership is free of charge and non-exclusive for both sides. No revenue share, referral fees or payments are owed unless set out in a separate written agreement. You remain free to partner with other projects; we remain free to partner with your competitors.

If we ever launch paid partner tiers, they will have their own terms and prices shown at checkout (then the Refund Policy on dash.disc-tools.de applies to payments).

9. Term, duration & termination

  • Duration / Term: Partnership begins on our approval and runs for the duration set in the approval — this can be fixed-term (e.g. 2 weeks, 3 months, 1 year, 4 years) or indefinite / unlimited. The concrete duration is stated in the approval DM/email and on the partner profile (expires_at). Examples: Duration: ∞ Infinite = indefinite; Duration: 90 days = ends after 90 days. We choose the duration case-by-case at approval (standard often 90 days or indefinite for trusted partners). There is no claim to a specific duration.
  • Expiry: A fixed-term partnership ends automatically on the expiry date at 23:59 UTC without separate termination notice. Listing, Discord Partner role and perks are removed automatically. You may apply for renewal/extension via ticket/DM/email before expiry — we are not obliged to extend. An indefinite partnership runs until terminated under the rules below.
  • Early termination by you: At any time, for any reason — even within a fixed term — via DM to our team or email to info@disc-tools.de. We will remove your listing within 14 days. No refund/compensation even if fixed term not exhausted.
  • Ordinary termination by us: At any time, for any reason — even within a fixed term — with 14 days notice by Discord DM or email. No compensation, including for remaining fixed-term time.
  • Immediate termination without warning: We may suspend or remove a partner immediately, without prior notice or warning, and without liability if we have reasonable grounds, in particular:
    • Zero-tolerance moderation failure (§5.2): Failure to immediately ban users for sexualization of minors (e.g. “I love little kids” in a sexual context), CSAM, grooming, hate, extremism, or keeping/defending such users.
    • Any violation of Discord ToS / Community Guidelines, German law (StGB, JuSchG, etc.), or illegal content on your properties.
    • Security risk — token grabber, malware, phishing, compromised invite/website.
    • Mandatory advertisement breach (§5.1): Failure to publish our advertisement verbatim within 24 hours of approval, publishing a modified/translated/shortened version, publishing anything other than our ad, deleting or hiding the ad before the partnership ends, or posting it in a volatile chat channel where it is immediately pushed down (instead of a dedicated staff-only channel on your server where only staff can write — not all members).
    • Prolonged inactivity (>90 days offline without notice), impersonation, or spam/abuse using the partnership.
    • Serious or repeated breach of the ping-role obligation (§5.3) incl. deleting/recreating the role when empty, gaming the fallback, or failure to ping the required @Partnership role / @everyone per §5.1/§5.3 for the advertisement.
    • Material misrepresentation, fake members, purchased bots, or serious community complaints.
  • Effect: On any termination you must remove any “Disc-Tools Partner” badges/claims from your properties within 7 days. We will remove your card from /partner/; cached copies/search engines may linger briefly. For immediate terminations the Discord Partner role is revoked instantly and no re-application is guaranteed (re-application blocked for at least 90 days for §5.2 violations, permanent for CSAM).
  • No claim: Terminated partners have no claim to compensation, reinstatement, or reasons beyond the category of breach (we may give a category, e.g. “§5.2 violation”).

No warnings for zero-tolerance: For child-safety and hate violations we do not issue warnings, strikes or “please fix”. One un-banned “I love little kids” (sexual) = instant end of partnership.
