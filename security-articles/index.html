Account hacked right now?

Don't panic — act fast in this order. Every minute counts before the attacker locks you out:

  1. Change your password — Discord Settings → My Account → Change Password.
  2. Log out all sessions — Settings → Devices → “Log Out All Known Devices”. This kills stolen tokens.
  3. Check Authorized Apps and remove everything unknown. Then follow the full Account Security Basics checklist.
