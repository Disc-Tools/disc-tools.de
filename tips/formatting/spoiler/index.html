Spoiler

Hide sensitive content behind double pipes. Users click to reveal.

Syntax

How it works

Examples

text|||Wrap text with two pipes on each side|
1

Basic Spoiler

||Spoiler content here||

Spoiler tags hide the content until clicked.

2

With other markup

||**Bold spoiler** *italic spoiler*~~strike~~||

You can combine spoiler with any other formatting.
