2. Hosting, CDN & Security

The Disc-Tools website at disc-tools.de and dashboard at dash.disc-tools.de are operated on server infrastructure hosted by Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland. Content delivery and DDoS protection are provided through Cloudflare. All connections are encrypted via TLS.

The bot hosting panel at hosting.disc-tools.de is part of the Disc-Tools service and is protected by Cloudflare. Bot files and related hosting data are stored on the server node assigned to the hosting account. The infrastructure provider and location for a bot server may differ from the website and panel infrastructure; contact info@disc-tools.de for details about the infrastructure used for your assigned service.

3. Server Logs

Our web servers and APIs, including the hosting panel, log requests such as your IP address, timestamp, HTTP method, requested path, HTTP status code, user agent, and error messages. Website and panel access logs are automatically rotated and kept for a maximum of 30 days. Additional technical records needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot bot servers may be kept for the period necessary for those purposes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in operating the service securely and detecting abuse.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site uses a cookie banner that asks for your consent before optional storage. Categories:

  • Essential: Always active. Needed for login and for storing your consent choice itself. Without them the service cannot work.
  • Analytics: Only stored after you accept. Helps us understand how the tools are used. We use a self-hosted instance of Umami Analytics on our own domain (umami.disc-tools.de) for website analytics. Umami is privacy-focused: it does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and all data is anonymized. No data is shared with third parties.
  • Preferences: Only stored after you accept. Stores favorites and settings on your device.

Favorites are stored in your browser's local storage and only on your device. This is strictly necessary for the favorites feature you explicitly requested. Your consent choice is stored in local storage so the banner is not shown again; you can change your choice at any time via the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Legal basis for optional cookies: your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

5. Discord login (OAuth2)

Discord OAuth2 is used for dashboard sign-in and for access requests to bot hosting. Depending on the flow, Discord provides your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar information and, for hosting requests, your email address. The hosting flow also asks Discord to verify your email and checks your membership in the required Discord server using the guilds.members.read scope. We use the membership check to determine eligibility; we do not store a copy of your Discord password.

These data are used to authenticate you, review a hosting access request, create and link an approved panel account, and provide account features. The legal basis for an access request and account is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request before entering into or performing a service); security and abuse prevention are based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You may disconnect your Discord account in the dashboard where that option is available, or contact us to exercise your data protection rights.

When you use Discord OAuth2, Discord Inc. and its subsidiaries process data under their own privacy policy. Please also read Discord's privacy policy.

6. Tools and data you submit

Most tools run in your browser. Data you enter (for example a user ID, an invite code or a color) is processed to answer your request and is not stored unless stated otherwise on the tool page.

For bot hosting, we process the information needed to review and administer your request: Discord ID, username and display name, avatar reference, email address, first and last name, preferred email language, request status, review timestamps and, if applicable, a rejection reason and reviewer. If approved, the account is linked to your Discord ID and uses the Discord username as its panel username. We use your email address to send request updates and an account password-setup link. The panel password you set is stored as a one-way password hash; we do not receive your Discord password.

When you use a bot server, files, configuration, databases and backups that you create or upload are stored on the server node assigned to you. We process this content to provide hosting, backups where enabled, support, security and abuse prevention. Authorized administrators may access hosting data when reasonably necessary for those purposes or to respond to a legal obligation. The hosting infrastructure provider may process technical and server data to operate that infrastructure.

Application and account data are processed to respond to your request and provide the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Hosting security, service administration and abuse prevention are based on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Email delivery providers receive the recipient address and message data needed to deliver service emails.

7. Retention periods

We retain account data while the account is active and retain hosting access requests for as long as needed to review and administer the request, prevent duplicate or abusive requests, provide an approved service, and establish or defend legal claims. Hosting files, configurations, databases and backups are retained while the corresponding server or backup is active; deletion and backup rotation may take additional time. Access logs are kept as described above. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing info@disc-tools.de. We handle verified deletion requests within 30 days where deletion is not prevented by a legal obligation or a necessary security or legal-claims retention period; data in backups may remain until the ordinary rotation cycle expires.
